01 / Research

Questions I want to pursue.

My research direction is centered on mechanics-led, patient-specific models that translate medical images and physiological data into useful cardiovascular insight.

Research objective

Develop computational methods that preserve physical meaning while becoming faster, more individualized, and more practical for clinical assessment.

Core directions

01

Cardiovascular biomechanics

How flow, arterial geometry, tissue behavior, and local stresses interact in vascular disease.

02

Patient-specific CFD

Image-derived vascular models that preserve clinically relevant anatomy and hemodynamics.

03

Cardiovascular digital twins

Individualized models connected with physiological data for prediction and longitudinal assessment.

04

Physics-informed learning

Physical constraints combined with machine learning for efficiency and interpretability.
