Undergraduate thesis2025–Present

Prediction of Left Coronary Artery Plaque Rupture Risk using CCTA-based One-Way FSI

Developed a patient-specific framework combining ANSYS Fluent and Transient Structural analysis on CCTA-derived coronary geometries reconstructed in Materialise Mimics and 3-matic. Multi-layer plaque components were modeled as distinct bodies to investigate mechanical vulnerability and stress distribution.
